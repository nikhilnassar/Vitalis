**Who we are.** Vitalis is developed by [YOUR NAME OR COMPANY], contact: [YOUR EMAIL].

## What Vitalis reads
With your permission, Vitalis reads from Apple Health (iOS) or Health Connect (Android): heart rate, heart-rate variability, resting heart rate, respiratory rate, sleep, steps, active calories, VO2 max, exercise sessions, weight, body fat, lean/water/bone mass, height and blood pressure. You choose which of these to allow and can revoke access at any time in the Apple Health or Health Connect settings.

## What you can type in
Profile (age, sex, height, weight), blood pressure and weight entries, daily check-ins (how you feel, habits) and blood-test results.

## How it is used
Only to calculate the recovery, strain, sleep, body, blood-pressure and trend screens inside the app, and to schedule the notifications you turn on. All of this happens **on your device**.

## What leaves your device
Nothing, by default. Vitalis has no account system and no servers that receive your health data. We do not sell or share your data, and we do not use it for advertising or to build profiles.

**Optional crash reports.** If you switch on "Share anonymous crash reports" in Settings, the app sends the error type, app version and device model/OS version to our crash-reporting provider (Sentry). These reports are scrubbed of health data, names, emails and IP-based location. They are off by default and you can turn them off at any time.

## Notifications
If you enable them, Vitalis schedules notifications on your device. Summaries may show your recovery score on your lock screen.

## Storage and deletion
Data you type in is stored in the app's private storage on your phone. **Settings > Delete all my data** removes it; uninstalling the app also removes it. Data inside Apple Health or Health Connect belongs to those apps and is not deleted by Vitalis.

## Not medical advice
Vitalis is not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any condition, and its estimates (recovery, strain, biological age, blood-pressure trend) are informational. Ask a clinician about health decisions. In an emergency, contact your local emergency number.

## Children
Vitalis is not intended for children under 16.
